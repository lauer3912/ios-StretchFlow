### 5. StretchGoGo (ios-StretchFlow) — v3.2.0 (Build 19)
**What's New in Version 3.2.0:**

- **Audio Coach Refinements**: Crystal-clear voice pacing with ducking support for background music playback.
- **Refined Profile & History**: Visual streak tracking and localized data export for physical therapy review.
